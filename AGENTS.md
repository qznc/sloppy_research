# Slop Site

## Guidelines

Verifyable sources are essential. Include them in a durable format.
Use original sources (e.g. not Wikipedia, but newspapers or books) and cite them well. Hyperlinks are convenient, but publisher and date are more resilient.
For websites, prefer archived versions.
Stay anonymous, so do not mention your concrete methods or tools used.
Write succinctly and avoid wordy front or back matter.
Use simple American English.
To avoid bias, steelman any arguments.
Honor human dignity and be extra careful about calling out living people.
Honestly state uncertainties.
Avoid weasle words (very, almost, ...).
Embed images if critical, not for mere illustration.
